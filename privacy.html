Privacy Policy

Last updated August 28, 2026

These apps do not collect any data. They make no network connections of any kind. Nothing you type, choose, or score ever leaves your Apple TV.

Which apps this covers

  • Menu Board
  • Stay Screen
  • Stage Timer
  • Picture Timer
  • Couch Scoreboard

What is stored, and where

Everything you set up is written to your Apple TV and stays there. It is not sent anywhere, not backed up to a server we run, and not readable by us.

AppWhat it keeps on your device
Menu BoardYour item names, prices, layout, and currency symbol
Stay ScreenThe details you enter for guests, including the Wi‑Fi name and password you choose to display
Stage TimerTalk length and warning thresholds
Picture TimerRoutine names and durations
Couch ScoreboardPlayer names and colors

Deleting the app removes all of it.

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts

  • No analytics or crash reporting service is used.
  • No advertising identifier is read. No tracking of any kind takes place.
  • There is no sign‑in, no account, and no email address is collected.
  • No third‑party SDKs are included. The apps use Apple frameworks only.

Purchases

Each app offers one optional in‑app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, and we receive no personal information from Apple about who bought what.

Children

Picture Timer is intended to be set up by a parent and watched by a child. Because the app collects nothing and connects to nothing, there is no children's data to protect, disclose, or delete.

Changes

If a future version of an app adds anything that connects to a network, this page will be updated before that version is submitted, and the App Store privacy details will be updated with it.

This policy applies to the App Store apps listed above and to no other product or service.
