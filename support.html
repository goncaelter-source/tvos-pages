Common questions

I bought the full version and it is asking me to buy it again.

Open the app's setup screen and choose Restore purchase. This happens after an Apple TV is reset or replaced, and restoring costs nothing. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

The app says the store could not be reached.

The App Store was unavailable when the app asked for the price. Everything free keeps working. Check the Apple TV's network connection and reopen the app.

The screen went dark or turned off on its own.

That is the Apple TV's own screen saver, set in Settings → General → Screen Saver → Start After. The apps that are meant to stay up hold it off while they are running.

My text is cut off at the bottom of the screen.

Every list scrolls. Swipe down on the remote's touch surface to reach the rest.

Menu Board: an item I added is not on the board.

Items with no name are left off. Open setup and give the row a name.

Stay Screen: the sample details are not mine.

The app opens with example content so you can see how it looks. Press the Menu button and replace each field with your own.

Couch Scoreboard: I need to reset one player's score.

Press Play/Pause to pause the game. Reset and Remove appear for the player currently selected.

The sound is too loud for the room.

There is a Sound switch on the setup screen of every app that makes one.

Requesting a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com.
